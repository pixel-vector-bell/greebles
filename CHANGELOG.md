# Greebles — What's New

## 0.2.0 — Catch & Collect
- The Wilds: Greebles now wander into the wilds on their own, even when the game is closed. Up to 6 wait at once and each stays about 2 hours, so coming back after a while means a bunch to catch in a row.
- Catch them yourself: tap a Greeble, pick a snare and throw it when the ring is small. Nice, Great and Perfect throws catch better. Rarer Greebles are harder to catch and may run away.
- Snares (Snare, Good, Great and the rare Star Snare that never misses) and Glowmoss Treats that calm a Greeble down.
- Hot spots: Glow Craters light up at random times for 30 minutes with lots of Greebles. Robot wrecks: beat the robot and Greebles come out of hiding. Beacons: 30 minutes of fast Greebles anywhere.
- Day, night and storms. Some Greebles come out more at night or in storms, and something very rare only falls on a stormy night…
- New rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic.
- ✨ Prismatic Greebles: about 1 in 400 sparkle with rainbow colors and are stronger.
- Quality stars (1 to 5) on every Greeble. Graft a copy in for a chance at more stars.
- 45 kinds in families. Most evolve by level, some need an item (Ember Core, Tide Pearl, Sun or Moon Prism) or only evolve at night. Glass Greebles can become two different things.
- A shop for snares, treats, Beacons and evolution items. Robots now drop snares and sometimes evolution items.
- The Field Guide shows each family and gives rewards as you catch more kinds: snares, bag space and more camp spots (up to 8).
- Your Greebles from 0.1 came with you as 3★, and you got a pack of snares to start.

## 0.1.0 — Greebles joins the game center
- You are an explorer stranded on a strange planet. Walk the planet every 5 minutes and a Greeble might follow you home.
- Put up to 4 Greebles in camp. They earn scrap and xp every 15 seconds, even while the game is closed (up to 8 hours).
- Graft two of the same Greeble to make one much stronger. Ash, Tide, Scrap and Prime Diggers grow into a new form.
- Send Skirmish Greebles to break robots (drones, pylons, colossi) for scrap and xp.
- New: a field guide of every Greeble you've found, a "Welcome back" card, and short tips for new players.
- Your Greebles are saved to your account, so they show up on your phone and your computer.
