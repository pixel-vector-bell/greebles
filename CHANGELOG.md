# Greebles — What's New

## 0.12.0 — Tidier menus & a real graft choice
- 🗂️ Camp, Fight, Shop, Ship and the Guide each have a small menu at the top now (for example Workers / Nest / Upgrades), so you see one short page at a time instead of one very long one. A gold dot shows where something is ready.
- 🖥️ On very wide screens, the right side used to spill cards off into hidden columns you had to slide sideways to reach. That's fixed: it's one straight column now.
- 🧬 Grafting lets you choose now. Tap "Graft…", pick the Greeble to keep, then pick the copy to feed in. Before you confirm, it shows the new level and star odds, and it warns you if you're about to feed in the stronger one or a Prismatic.
- 🤖 The three robot buttons on Patrol sit in one even row instead of wrapping.

## 0.11.1 — Five more touch-ups
- 🎨 Tide Pup now wears a yellow raincoat and boots and no longer looks see-through.
- 🎨 Drift Greeble is paler with a cloud on its head, so it's easy to tell apart from Tide Pup.
- 🎨 Ash Pup looks like a little baby now, and Ash Greeble is a tall miner with an axe, so the two are easy to tell apart.
- 🎨 Rift Pup lost the swirl on its belly.

## 0.11.0 — Every Greeble looks like a Greeble
- 🎨 61 of the 72 Greebles have been redrawn. Before, many looked like little dragons or bats, with wings, bat ears or lizard tails. Now every one is clearly the same goblin species: pointed goblin ears, a flat face and a big grin.
- 🧢 Each family has its own outfit or prop, so they're easier to tell apart: miner lamps, raincoats, pirate hats, boxing gloves, headphones, crowns and more.
- 🚫 No more look-alikes of other games' creatures. The fire, electric and crystal families all got new designs.

## 0.10.0 — Desktop view, Start over & new backgrounds
- 🖥️ Desktop view: on a PC or a wide tablet, the Wilds stay on the left all the time while you use the other tabs on the right. Tabs move to the top, text is bigger, and the catch screen opens as a centered panel. Phones look the same as before.
- 🔄 Start over: Settings → "Start over (erase progress)". It shows exactly what you'd lose, then needs a second tap after a 2-second wait, so you can't do it by accident. It wipes the save on every device, and the walkthrough starts again.
- 🎨 New painted backgrounds for Glass Spires (a bright crystal valley) and the Void Rift (a pastel purple plain), so all 5 areas match now. Greebles get a soft outline so they stand out on every background.

## 0.9.0 — Eggs, daily tasks & a fresh look
- 🪺 Nest & eggs (Camp tab): put two Greebles from the same family in the nest and they lay an egg about every 2 hours, even while you're away. Eggs hatch over time, and every Greeble you catch warms them by 10 minutes. Eggs often have more stars, and they're 3× more likely to be ✨ Prismatic (9× if a parent is Prismatic). The incubator holds 3 eggs.
- 📋 Daily tasks (Wilds tab): 3 small goals every day, like "Catch 4 Tide Greebles" or "Land 3 Perfect throws". Each one pays scrap and Good Snares, and finishing all 3 gives a bonus of 2 Great Snares and a Shiny Bait.
- 🎨 Each area has its own painted background now, and it gets darker at night and in storms.
- ✨ Evolving and hatching have a bright flash, and ready eggs wiggle.
- 👋 New players get a short walkthrough: catch your first Greeble, put it in camp, and find your daily tasks. You can skip it, and it's off for anyone who already plays.

## 0.8.0 — Mythic Hunts, the Void Rift & sound
- 🌠 Mythic Hunts! There are now 5 Mythic Greebles, and each has its own hunt with 3 goals (like "Catch 25 Tide Greebles" or "Break 40 patrol robots"). Finish a hunt to get a Star Snare and call its Mythic into its area. If it gets away, you can call it again. Hunts are at the top of the Guide tab, and progress counts across all your trips home.
- 🕳️ A 5th area: the Void Rift. Beat the Prism Sentinel to open it. It has 7 new Greebles (the Rift, Glimmer and Echo families), a new Legendary (the Rift Sovereign) and a new boss, the Null Engine.
- 🔊 Sound effects: throws, wobbles, catches, Prismatic and Mythic sparkles, robot fights, evolving, boss wins and more. You can turn them off in Settings.
- 🤖 The patrol robots (drone, pylon and colossus) and the new boss have real art now.
- The Field Guide has 73 kinds and a new reward at 65 caught.

## 0.7.0 — Real Greeble art
- Every Greeble now has its own picture! All 60 kinds got cut-out sticker art that sits nicely on the dark screens, plus the 4 robot bosses.
- ✨ Prismatic Greebles look special now: a rainbow ring, colors that shift, and a sparkle that sweeps across them.
- The Ash element has a new icon (🌋).

## 0.6.0 — The long game
- Your ship! A new 🚀 Ship tab. Fix your crashed ship in 5 steps (hull, engine, fuel, antenna and nav core). Each step costs scrap and materials, and the later ones need you to beat the bosses.
- Signal home: once the ship is fixed, call home to start a new trip. You keep your Field Guide, Prismatic marks, perks and 3 Greebles of your choice (at half level). Everything else starts fresh, and the robots get tougher each trip, but they pay more too.
- Signal Points: every trip home earns points (more for kinds caught, boss wins and Prismatics). Spend them on 6 perks that last forever: better catch chance, more Prismatics, more camp scrap, a care package of snares, more Greebles waiting in the wilds, and longer away time.
- Daily expedition: once a day, send one Greeble off for 4 hours. It comes back with a big pile of loot, and a rare Greeble follows it home.
- Weekly swarm: each week one kind of Greeble shows up everywhere, in every area.

## 0.5.0 — Jobs & bait
- Camp jobs: campers now bring things back. Dig Greebles find 🪨 Ore, Trawl Greebles pull in 🌱 Kelp, and Skirmish Greebles salvage ⚙️ Gears. Strong Dig Greebles in the Glass Spires sometimes find rare 🔷 Crystal. This keeps going while you're away.
- Camp moves with you. Each area favours one caste: Dig in Ash Flats and Glass Spires, Trawl in Tide Pools, Skirmish in Rust Fields. Favoured campers (⭐) find things 1.5× more and earn 25% more scrap.
- Bait: craft Dig, Trawl or Skirmish Bait to bring out lots of that caste for 30 minutes, or Shiny Bait for 3× rare Greebles and 3× Prismatic odds.
- The Workbench has new recipes that use Ore, Kelp and Crystal, including Great Snares, treats and evolution items.
- The welcome-back card shows what your campers brought back.

## 0.4.0 — Battles that matter
- Elements! Every Greeble and robot has one of 8 elements: Ash, Tide, Ember, Moss, Glass, Rust, Storm and Void. Each element hits two others 1.5× harder. There's a chart on the Fight tab.
- Special moves: in a fight, each Greeble charges up a move (like Riptide, Flare Burst or Thunderclap). Tap it when it glows for a 3× hit. If you don't, it fires on its own at 2×.
- Any Greeble can fight robots now, not just Skirmish ones (Skirmish still hit hardest). The fight buttons show ⬆ when your element is strong and ⬇ when it's weak.
- Robots drop ⚙️ Robot Gears, and big robots and bosses drop 🔋 Power Cores. Craft snares, treats, Beacons, evolution items and even Star Snares at the new Workbench in the Shop.
- The win check now counts elements and moves too.

## 0.3.0 — Areas, bosses & camp
- A map! Four areas to explore: Ash Flats, Tide Pools, Rust Fields and Glass Spires. Each has its own Greebles, and wild Greebles are higher level the further you go.
- Every area has a robot boss. Pick a team of up to 3 Greebles to fight it (Skirmish Greebles hit hardest). Beat it to open the next area.
- Beating a boss calls out that area's Legendary Greeble, and from then on it can roam there. The Glass Spires has a brand new one: the Crystal Sovereign.
- Bosses can be fought again every 2 hours for more scrap, Great Snares and Beacons.
- Robots get tougher (and pay more) in later areas.
- 16 new Greebles: Shell, Coral, Bolt, Cog, Shard and Glow families, plus the Crystal Sovereign. 60 kinds in all.
- Build your camp with scrap: extra tents (up to 8 spots), a bigger bag, a Night Watch so camp earns longer while you're away (up to 24 hours), a Workbench for more scrap, and a Snare Press that makes free snares.
- New Field Guide rewards at 50, 55 and 60 kinds.

## 0.2.0 — Catch & Collect
- The Wilds: Greebles now wander into the wilds on their own, even when the game is closed. Up to 6 wait at once and each stays about 2 hours, so coming back after a while means a bunch to catch in a row.
- Catch them yourself: tap a Greeble, pick a snare and throw it when the ring is small. Nice, Great and Perfect throws catch better. Rarer Greebles are harder to catch and may run away.
- Snares (Snare, Good, Great and the rare Star Snare that never misses) and Glowmoss Treats that calm a Greeble down.
- Hot spots: Glow Craters light up at random times for 30 minutes with lots of Greebles. Robot wrecks: beat the robot and Greebles come out of hiding. Beacons: 30 minutes of fast Greebles anywhere.
- Day, night and storms. Some Greebles come out more at night or in storms, and something very rare only falls on a stormy night…
- New rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic.
- ✨ Prismatic Greebles: about 1 in 400 sparkle with rainbow colors and are stronger.
- Quality stars (1 to 5) on every Greeble. Graft a copy in for a chance at more stars.
- 45 kinds in families. Most evolve by level, some need an item (Ember Core, Tide Pearl, Sun or Moon Prism) or only evolve at night. Glass Greebles can become two different things.
- A shop for snares, treats, Beacons and evolution items. Robots now drop snares and sometimes evolution items.
- The Field Guide shows each family and gives rewards as you catch more kinds: snares, bag space and more camp spots (up to 8).
- Your Greebles from 0.1 came with you as 3★, and you got a pack of snares to start.

## 0.1.0 — Greebles joins the game center
- You are an explorer stranded on a strange planet. Walk the planet every 5 minutes and a Greeble might follow you home.
- Put up to 4 Greebles in camp. They earn scrap and xp every 15 seconds, even while the game is closed (up to 8 hours).
- Graft two of the same Greeble to make one much stronger. Ash, Tide, Scrap and Prime Diggers grow into a new form.
- Send Skirmish Greebles to break robots (drones, pylons, colossi) for scrap and xp.
- New: a field guide of every Greeble you've found, a "Welcome back" card, and short tips for new players.
- Your Greebles are saved to your account, so they show up on your phone and your computer.
